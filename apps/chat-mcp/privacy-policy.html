Privacy Policy for ChatMCP

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information ChatMCP ("the App") uses, where that information goes, and the choices you have. It applies to ChatMCP on iOS (from the Apple App Store) and on Android (from Google Play). ChatMCP is made by Kitsune Apps, LLC ("Kitsune Apps," "we," "us," or "our").

Please also read our Terms of Service.

The Short Version

  • ChatMCP has no accounts. There is no sign-up or login, and we don't run a server that stores your chats.
  • Your chats, settings and API keys are stored on your device.
  • Your messages, and any device data the assistant uses to answer you, are sent directly from your device to the AI provider you choose, using your own API key. That provider's privacy policy applies to what it receives.
  • ChatMCP reads your contacts, calendar, location, photos and other device data only when you ask it to, and only after you grant permission.
  • We use Google Firebase Analytics to collect usage events. These are linked to a random ID, not to your name. They never include the content of your messages or your API keys.
  • There are no ads, we don't use the Advertising ID, and we don't sell your data.

1. How ChatMCP Works

ChatMCP is an AI chat app. You connect your own AI provider with your own API key. The App supports Anthropic (Claude), Google (Gemini), OpenAI, OpenRouter, and "Custom" providers: OpenAI-compatible servers that you run or choose yourself, for example a local Ollama server. On iOS, you can also use Apple's on-device model, which processes your messages on your device and sends nothing off it.

The assistant can use tools on your device (for example, to look up a contact or a calendar event) and tools on remote MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers that you add. Some MCP servers ask you to sign in with them in your browser.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The following stays on your device and is not sent to us:

  • Your chat history
  • Your settings, including the providers and MCP servers you add
  • Your API keys, which are kept in your platform's secure storage. An API key is only sent to the provider it belongs to.

Deleting a chat removes it from the App. Clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App deletes all of it. Information that has already been sent to an AI provider or MCP server is covered by that service's policy (see Section 4).

3. Device Features ChatMCP Can Use

The App uses these features only when you ask the assistant to do something that needs them. Each one is behind your operating system's permission prompt, and you can turn it off at any time (see Section 9).

  • Contacts: read, for example to find someone's phone number or email address.
  • Calendar: read your events, and create, edit or delete events when you ask.
  • Reminders (iOS only): to work with your reminders when you ask.
  • Location: your current location, only while you are using the App. ChatMCP never accesses your location in the background.
  • Photos: your recent photos, or photos from a date range, including the location stored in each photo (EXIF data). Attaching a picture to a message uses the system photo picker.
  • Camera: to take a picture through your device's camera app.
  • Microphone: for dictation, using your operating system's speech recognizer. The App receives only the transcribed text.
  • Messages, email and phone calls: the App only opens your device's messaging app, email composer or phone dialer with fields filled in. You decide whether to send or call. ChatMCP never reads your text messages, emails or call history.
  • Text to speech: uses your operating system's voice engine.

4. Where Your Information Goes

4.1 The AI provider you choose

Every time you send a message, the App sends the following directly from your device to the AI provider you selected, using your API key:

  • Your message, whether typed or dictated
  • Any images you attach
  • The system prompt (the instructions the App gives the model)
  • The results of any tools the assistant used to answer you. This can include contact details, calendar events, your location, photos and their location, weather, and the current time.

Contact details may include information about other people (such as their names, phone numbers and email addresses). Please consider this before asking the assistant about your contacts.

This information does not pass through our servers. Each provider handles it under its own privacy policy and terms, which may allow it to keep your data for some time and, depending on your plan or settings with that provider, to use it to improve its services. Please review them:

  • Anthropic: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • Google (Gemini): https://policies.google.com/privacy and the Gemini API terms at https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms
  • OpenAI: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • OpenRouter: https://openrouter.ai/privacy. OpenRouter forwards your requests to the model provider you pick, whose own policy also applies.
  • Custom providers: the server you enter receives the same information. Whoever runs that server is responsible for it.
  • Apple's on-device model (iOS only): nothing is sent off your device.

4.2 MCP servers you add

When the assistant uses a tool from an MCP server you added, the App sends that server the tool-call details the model chooses. These can include content from your conversation. If a server requires sign-in, you sign in with that server in your browser. MCP servers are run by their own operators, not by us, and each operator's privacy policy applies. Only add servers you trust.

4.3 Analytics (Google Firebase Analytics)

The App uses Firebase Analytics to help us understand how ChatMCP is used and to find problems. It sends:

  • Usage events: app opened; message sent (the model name and the message's length, not its content); message failed to send (including the error text); tool used (the tool's name); MCP server added or edited (the server's name and a hash of its URL); paywall shown; purchase-mode settings changed; and AI response reported (the reason, provider, model and response length).
  • Device information: your device model and operating system version.
  • Identifiers: a Firebase app-instance ID, and a random ID that the App generates and sets as your analytics user ID.
  • Standard Firebase data: Firebase also collects some information automatically, such as first opens, sessions and basic diagnostics. On Android, this can include the Google Play install referrer (how you found the App). See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

This data is pseudonymous, not anonymous: it isn't linked to your name or email address, but the random user ID stays the same each time you use the App, so events from your device can be linked to each other. Analytics never includes the content of your messages, your attachments or your API keys. The App doesn't currently have a setting to turn analytics off.

4.4 App integrity (Google Firebase App Check)

To protect our backend services from abuse, the App uses Firebase App Check. When the App launches, it may ask your device's integrity service (Play Integrity on Android, DeviceCheck on iOS) to confirm that it is a genuine copy of the App on a genuine device. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

4.5 Purchases (RevenueCat, the App Store and Google Play)

We use RevenueCat to manage in-app purchases. When you view the paywall, buy or restore Pro Access, or the App checks whether you have Pro Access, RevenueCat and the store you use (the Apple App Store or Google Play Billing) process an anonymous RevenueCat user ID, purchase receipts or tokens, and your purchase history. Payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your payment card details. See:

  • RevenueCat: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4.6 Weather (OpenWeatherMap)

The weather tool is off by default. If you turn it on and the assistant uses it, the App sends coordinates or a city name to OpenWeatherMap to get the forecast. See https://openweather.co.uk/privacy-policy.

4.7 Speech recognition and text to speech

When you dictate, your audio goes to your operating system's speech recognizer (Apple's on iOS, Google's on Android), which may process it on your device or on Apple's or Google's servers under their privacy policies. ChatMCP receives only the transcribed text. Reading responses aloud uses your operating system's voice engine.

4.8 Emails you send us

If you send us feedback, or report an AI response, the App opens your email app with a message to feedback@kitsuneapps.com. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself. A report email is filled in with the text of the response you reported, the provider and model names, and the App and device versions; you can review and change it before sending. We receive whatever you choose to send, and use it to respond to you and to improve the App.

4.9 Legal requirements

We may disclose the limited information we hold (such as analytics data or emails you've sent us) if required by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

5. What We Don't Do

  • ChatMCP has no ads and contains no advertising SDKs.
  • We don't use your device's advertising identifier (the IDFA on iOS or the Advertising ID on Android), and we never ask to track you across other companies' apps or websites. On Android, Advertising ID collection is explicitly disabled.
  • We don't sell your data or share it with data brokers.
  • We don't store your chats, attachments or API keys on our servers.
  • We don't read your text messages, emails or call history.

6. Retention and Deletion

  • On your device: chats, settings and API keys stay until you delete chats, clear the App's data or uninstall the App.
  • Analytics: Firebase deletes detailed analytics data automatically after no more than 14 months. Because this data isn't linked to your name or email address, we usually can't tell which data is yours. If you have a question or a deletion request, email feedback@kitsuneapps.com and we'll do what we can.
  • Emails you send us: kept for as long as we need them to respond to you and improve the App, then deleted. You can ask us to delete them at any time.
  • AI providers, MCP servers and other services: their retention is set by their own policies (see Section 4).

7. Security

API keys are kept in your platform's secure storage. Traffic between the App and the built-in AI providers, Firebase, RevenueCat and OpenWeatherMap uses encrypted HTTPS connections.

If you add a Custom provider with an address that starts with http:// instead of https:// (for example, a server on your home network), traffic to that server, including your messages and tool results, is not encrypted. Use https:// whenever the server supports it.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

ChatMCP is intended for adults 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your Choices and Permissions

  • On iOS: open the Settings app, go to Apps → ChatMCP (on older iOS versions, scroll down to ChatMCP), and turn Contacts, Calendars, Reminders, Location, Photos, Camera, Microphone and Speech Recognition on or off. You can also manage them under Settings → Privacy & Security.
  • On Android: open Settings → Apps → ChatMCP → Permissions, and allow or deny Contacts, Calendar, Location, Photos and videos, Camera and Microphone. (The exact menu names can vary by device.)
  • In the App: you can turn individual tools off so the assistant can't use them, choose which AI provider receives your messages, and remove MCP servers.
  • Deleting your data: delete chats in the App, or clear the App's data or uninstall it to remove everything stored on your device. To delete analytics data, see Section 6. To delete data held by an AI provider or MCP server, contact that service.

10. International Users and Your Rights

The services described in Section 4 may process information in countries other than the one where you live, including the United States. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to how it is used. To make a request about data we control (analytics data and emails you've sent us), contact us using the details below.

We use analytics because we have a legitimate interest in understanding how the App is used and fixing problems. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are used in California law.
